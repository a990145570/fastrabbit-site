How it works

Send the address. Get the sky back.

No mystery process. Four steps, then you have stills that make a property look like itself — only taller.

  1. Enquire

    Tell us the address, the service you need, and your preferred window. We’ll confirm whether the site is a fit for Basic operations.

  2. Schedule

    We pick a flyable day together. Winter weather is picky; spring is friendlier. No travel fee inside Northumberland.

  3. Fly

    A shows up with the DJI Lito X1, shoots the set from planned angles, and keeps clear of people on the ground.

  4. Deliver

    Edited stills in about 24 hours for listings and roofs; builder progress packages within about 48 hours.

About Fast Rabbit

One pilot. One Lito. Cobourg as home base.

Fast Rabbit is A’s aerial photography practice for Northumberland County — Cobourg, Port Hope, Grafton, Baltimore, Colborne, Campbellford, and nearby towns where the airspace cooperates.

The aircraft is a DJI Lito X1. The product lineup is deliberately short: listing aerials, roof photo sets, and (from spring 2027) builder progress visits. First paid work is planned for January 2027 at the earliest.

Company setup, registration, and related paperwork are still in progress. This site is a draft for design and copy — nothing here is a claim that Fast Rabbit is open for business today.
