Community

Our neighbours come first.

FastRabbit calls Cobourg home, and our community and non-profit work sits right up front, next to everything else we do. We love the lakeshore towns, the halls and churches that host the bazaars, and the volunteers who keep the festivals going, and we want to give something back to the places that make this county feel like home.

Workshops and community

At FastRabbit, being part of Cobourg, Port Hope and Northumberland County means being present in the community, building a reputation we can stand behind, and doing work that is worth the time we give it. We offer workshops for senior high school students who are interested in drones. The first talk is free, and students who want to go further can join us for ongoing development. We weigh goodwill against time invested: we would rather be known for showing up well than for speaking to a room and leaving. If you teach a senior class, or lead a community or civic group, and would like to talk, please write to sales@fastrabbit.org.

About FastRabbit

Cobourg as home base.

Hello from Cobourg. We’re FastRabbit, and we make aerial photography across Northumberland County, Ontario — mostly real-estate listings, plus other jobs where the airspace cooperates.

Our lineup starts with listing aerials, roof photo sets and close-up roof and chimney photos, night beauty shots, property checks, After the Storm records, Private Gatherings, Pet Find and (from spring 2027) builder progress visits, and now reaches to Scheduled Condition Photos, 360° aerial panoramas, overhead site maps and 3D models, live view from above, and photos and films for farms, shorelines, trades, marinas, resorts and towns. Consent comes first on every job. To talk about a property, our contact details are in the booking section below.

How it works

Send the address. Get the sky back.

No mystery process. Four steps, then you have stills that make a property look like itself — only taller.

  1. Enquire

    Tell us the address, the service you need, and your preferred window. We’ll confirm whether the site is a good fit before we book.

  2. Schedule

    We pick a flyable day together. Winter weather is picky; spring is friendlier.

  3. Fly

    We arrive, shoot the set from planned angles, and work safely around people, coming in close only with their consent.

  4. Deliver

    Edited stills in about 24 hours for listings and roofs; builder progress packages within about 48 hours.

Every job comes with a free flight record: the date, time, place and conditions of your flight, kept on file and yours on request.

On request, you get a short dated summary of each flight we made for you, handy for your own files, insurer or contractor.
