Services & pricing

Everything Fast Rabbit offers on the DJI Lito X1 — listing aerials, a video clip add-on, cottage and acreage sets, roof photo sets, and builder progress visits. Founding prices apply to early jobs; standard prices are the ongoing card.

Leads winter · from Jan 2027

Listing aerials — town lot

10–15 edited 48 MP stills, approximate lot lines, optional clip. Typical delivery about 24 hours. Built for MLS-style listing packages across Cobourg and nearby towns.

  • 10–15 edited stills from planned angles
  • Approximate lot lines (visual guide, not a survey)
  • Optional listing video clip add-on
  • ~24-hour turnaround after the flight
  • No travel fee inside Northumberland

  • Price reviewed after the first 5 jobs

Cottage / acreage listing

Same listing-aerial craft for bigger footprints — cottages, shoreline context, and acreage where the lot needs more than a town-lot pass. Pricing is estimated and may settle with real time-on-site data.

Second winter line · from Jan 2027

Roof photo sets

15–25 labelled photos plus a short clip. These are visual roof condition photos — never an inspection, never a measurement, never a claim about structural soundness.

  • 15–25 labelled stills covering roof surfaces
  • Short accompanying clip
  • ~24-hour delivery
  • Useful for restoration, ice-dam season, and roofing quotes
  • Some tight residential lots may be declined under Basic operations rules

Pricing

First roof per firm Free
Thereafter C$99per set · revisit with real time-on-site data

Wording matters: visual condition photos only. We do not call this an inspection.

Starts spring 2027 · Apr [est.]

Builder progress visits

Fixed photo points re-shot each visit — dated stills plus a short clip, typically within 48 hours. Designed for recurring monthly coverage once spring construction picks up. Not offered in winter.

  • Consistent angles across visits for easy comparison
  • Dated stills + short clip each visit
  • ~48-hour package delivery
  • Recurring monthly once underway
  • Useful for builders and construction lenders

Pricing

3-month pilot C$175per visit
Standard (est.) C$250per visit · or C$300/month [est.]

Not offered November–March. Spring start estimated April 2027.
