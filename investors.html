A letter from FastRabbit

Hello from Cobourg. We’re FastRabbit, a local aerial photography studio working across Northumberland County, Ontario — the towns, the farms, the shoreline, and the streets we already know.

We want to grow the way a neighbour would: by doing good work, keeping our word, and becoming a familiar name in this county. Relationships, community goodwill and awareness come first. We are not trying to be the cheapest option, and we will not build this business on a race to the bottom.

I’m opening this conversation because FastRabbit is looking for investment to grow. I also want to make connections, and to meet people I can collaborate with.

If you would like to talk, please write to Adam Lloyd at adam@fastrabbit.org.

Adam Lloyd, President & Managing Director, FastRabbit Aeronautics
