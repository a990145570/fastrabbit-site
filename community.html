A free first talk for students

Cobourg is home base for FastRabbit, and we would love to be a good neighbour here and across Port Hope and Northumberland County. If you are a senior high school student who is curious about drones, or a teacher with a class that is, we would be glad to come by for a free first talk about how drones work and what our corner of Ontario looks like from above. Students who catch the bug can keep learning with us afterwards. If your class, club or community group would like a visit, just write to sales@fastrabbit.org and we will take it from there.
